# ReCoVP-Hybrid 三图图注

## 图 1 两阶段恢复框架

**中文** 深蓝色主线从受扰观测 $\boldsymbol y$ 依次经过两次相减。第一阶段以观测的均方根幅值 $r$ 归一化输入，利用已知线性调频模板 $\boldsymbol p$、估计时延和学习窗构造 16 列局部字典 $\boldsymbol B$。逐原子证据 $\rho_k$ 参与确定对角正则矩阵 $\boldsymbol\Lambda$。变量投影求得系数 $\widehat{\boldsymbol c}$，从观测中减去干扰估计 $\widehat{\boldsymbol j}_1=r\boldsymbol B\widehat{\boldsymbol c}$，得到初步恢复 $\widehat{\boldsymbol u}_1$。第二阶段将初步恢复与干扰估计按 $r$ 归一化，输入复值 U-Net。赭色支路提供相位不变条件 $\boldsymbol\psi$。脉冲门结合条件特征统计量和脉冲级物理量，给出标量 $\alpha$。第二次相减扣除修正量 $\boldsymbol\delta_2=r\alpha\widetilde{\boldsymbol\delta}$，得到最终输出 $\widehat{\boldsymbol u}$。左下扫频线为模板示意。

**Figure 1. Two-stage recovery in ReCoVP-Hybrid.** The blue signal path carries the observation $\boldsymbol y$ through two subtractions. In the first stage, the observation is normalized by its root-mean-square amplitude $r$. Estimated delays and learned windows applied to the known linear frequency-modulated template $\boldsymbol p$ define the 16-column local dictionary $\boldsymbol B$. Atom-wise evidence $\rho_k$ informs the diagonal regularizer $\boldsymbol\Lambda$. Variable projection estimates the coefficients $\widehat{\boldsymbol c}$. Subtracting $\widehat{\boldsymbol j}_1=r\boldsymbol B\widehat{\boldsymbol c}$ from the observation gives the first-stage output $\widehat{\boldsymbol u}_1$. The complex U-Net takes the normalized pair of first-stage estimates and proposes a residual correction. The ochre path supplies phase-invariant features $\boldsymbol\psi$. A pulse gate combines feature summaries and pulse-level measurements to produce the scalar $\alpha$. Subtracting $\boldsymbol\delta_2=r\alpha\widetilde{\boldsymbol\delta}$ from $\widehat{\boldsymbol u}_1$ gives the final output $\widehat{\boldsymbol u}$. The small chirp denotes the known template schematically.

## 图 2 四级复值精修网络与条件门控残差块

**中文** (a) 复值 U-Net 通过三次平均池化和三次线性上采样连接四个时间分辨率。斜切板标示特征张量，尺寸依次为时间采样点数和复通道数。同分辨率的编码特征经水平跳接与上采样特征拼接。瓶颈串联三个扩张率分别为 1、2、4 的块，末端核长为 1 的复卷积输出修正提案 $\widetilde{\boldsymbol\delta}$。赭色箭头举例标出编码端和解码端的条件输入。(b) 放大首个编码层中含恒等跳连的残差块。无偏置复卷积将 $\boldsymbol z$ 映射为 $\boldsymbol v$。实值支路拼接稳定化对数幅值与相位不变特征 $\boldsymbol\psi$，经实卷积、SiLU 和 Sigmoid 产生正实值门 $\boldsymbol g$。该门逐点缩放 $\boldsymbol v$ 的幅值，保留其相位。输出为 $\boldsymbol z^{+}=\boldsymbol z+\boldsymbol g\odot\boldsymbol v$。

**Figure 2. Four-level complex refiner and its conditional residual block.** (a) Three average-pooling and three linear-upsampling steps connect four temporal resolutions. Tensor dimensions give the number of time samples followed by the number of complex channels. Each lateral skip concatenates encoder features with upsampled features at the matching resolution. Three bottleneck blocks use successive dilation rates of 1, 2 and 4. A final complex convolution of kernel length 1 produces the proposed correction $\widetilde{\boldsymbol\delta}$. The ochre arrows show examples of conditioning at the encoder and decoder. (b) The first encoder level illustrates a block with an identity skip. A bias-free complex convolution maps $\boldsymbol z$ to $\boldsymbol v$. The real branch concatenates the stabilized log magnitude of $\boldsymbol v$ with the phase-invariant features $\boldsymbol\psi$. A real convolution followed by SiLU and a sigmoid gives a positive real gate $\boldsymbol g$. The gate scales each complex feature without rotating its phase. Residual addition gives $\boldsymbol z^{+}=\boldsymbol z+\boldsymbol g\odot\boldsymbol v$.

## 图 3 单个仿真脉冲的匹配滤波响应与字典证据

**中文** (a) 三行依次显示同一脉冲的观测、第一阶段输出及最终输出与同一发射模板的匹配滤波幅值。三条曲线共用输入峰值作为 0 dB 参考，显示全部 1,201 个有效延迟单元，显示下限为 −65 dB。圆点标出各行全局峰值。输入峰位于延迟单元 278，两个恢复阶段的峰均位于单元 85，分别为 −14.78 和 −20.20 dB。两次恢复输出在同一峰位相差 5.42 dB。(b) 同一条记录的 16 个字典原子按证据 $\rho_k$ 降序排列。每行显示一个原子在局部时窗内的 15 点 Hann 加权均方根幅值，色标相对于完整字典的最大原始幅值。右侧横条给出对应原子的相对证据 $\rho_k/\max_j\rho_j$。

**Figure 3. Matched-filter response and dictionary evidence for one simulated pulse.** (a) The observation, first-stage output and final output are matched to the same transmit template. All three profiles use the input peak as their common 0 dB reference and show all 1,201 valid delay lags with a display floor of −65 dB. Dots mark the global peaks. The input peaks at lag 278. Both recovery outputs peak at lag 85, at −14.78 and −20.20 dB, respectively. The peak at this common output lag decreases by 5.42 dB between stages. (b) The 16 dictionary atoms from the same pulse are ranked by decreasing evidence $\rho_k$. Each row shows the local root-mean-square magnitude of one atom, computed with a 15-sample Hann window and displayed relative to the maximum unsmoothed magnitude of the full dictionary. Each bar gives the relative evidence $\rho_k/\max_j\rho_j$ for the atom on the same row.
